# Labour Market in Sweden

## Summary
Jobseeking often involves Arbetsförmedlingen, employers, and sometimes municipal or private actors. Workplace culture and contracts matter for newcomers.

## Key points for editors
- Do not promise employment outcomes.
- Distinguish public employment service from private recruiters.
