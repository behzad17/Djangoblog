# Taxation in Sweden

## Summary
Skatteverket administers taxes and related registration. Editors should explain concepts carefully and avoid tax advice.

## Key points for editors
- Separate tax declaration from population registration topics when possible.
- Prefer official terminology for A-skatt / F-skatt with glossary support.
