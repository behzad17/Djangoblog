# Försäkringskassan

## Purpose
Försäkringskassan administers national social insurance benefits such as parental benefits and sickness-related support.

## Responsibilities
- Parental leave benefits (föräldrapenning)
- Sickness benefit processes
- Certain disability and care-related benefits

## Common situations
- Parental leave planning
- Sick leave paperwork with employers

## Editorial wording
Keep the Swedish name **Försäkringskassan** in first mention; explain in plain language afterward if needed.

## Common misunderstandings
- Not a general “welfare office” for all municipal support (compare socialtjänsten / kommunen).
