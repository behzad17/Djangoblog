# Arbetsförmedlingen

## Purpose
Arbetsförmedlingen is the public employment service supporting jobseekers and employers.

## Responsibilities
- Job matching and vacancy information
- Programmes and support for jobseekers
- Cooperation with municipalities and other actors

## Common situations
- Registering as jobseeker
- Finding first job pathways
- Understanding programme requirements

## Editorial wording
Use **Arbetsförmedlingen**; do not confuse with private staffing agencies.

## Common misunderstandings
- Registration does not guarantee a job offer.
- Rules and programmes change—prefer official current guidance.
