# Kronofogden

## Purpose
Kronofogden is the Swedish Enforcement Authority dealing with debts, enforcement, and related registers.

## Responsibilities
- Debt enforcement
- Payment orders and related processes
- Public information about debt consequences

## Common situations
- Unpaid bills escalating to enforcement
- Understanding payment plans and consequences

## Editorial wording
Use **Kronofogden**. Be careful, precise, and non-alarmist.

## Common misunderstandings
- Not every late invoice automatically means Kronofogden involvement.
