# CSN

## Purpose
CSN administers student grants and loans for eligible studies.

## Responsibilities
- Study grants and loans
- Repayment administration
- Information on study support rules

## Common situations
- Applying for student finance
- Understanding repayment after studies

## Editorial wording
Prefer **CSN** with expansion “Centrala studiestödsnämnden” on first mention in long guides.

## Common misunderstandings
- Support depends on study type, pace, and personal circumstances—avoid absolute claims.
