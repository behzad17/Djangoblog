# Polisen

## Purpose
Polisen is Sweden's police authority responsible for public order, crime prevention, and certain permits.

## Responsibilities
- Emergency response and crime investigation
- Passports and national ID in relevant processes
- Public safety information

## Common situations
- Reporting a crime
- Passport applications
- Emergency number 112 awareness

## Editorial wording
Use **Polisen** for the authority; distinguish local police contacts from emergency services.

## Common misunderstandings
- 112 is for emergencies; non-urgent matters use other channels.
