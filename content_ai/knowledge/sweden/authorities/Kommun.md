# Kommun

## Purpose
A **kommun** is a Swedish municipality responsible for many local services such as schools, eldercare, and local planning.

## Responsibilities
- Preschool and compulsory school organisation
- Social services (socialtjänst)
- Local infrastructure and planning
- Some housing-related processes

## Common situations
- Registering a child for preschool
- Contacting social services
- Local waste, parking, and community services

## Editorial wording
Prefer “municipality (kommun)” on first mention. Name the specific municipality when known.

## Common misunderstandings
- Municipal services differ between municipalities—avoid nationwide absolutes.
