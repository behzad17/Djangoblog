# Skatteverket

## Purpose
Skatteverket is Sweden's tax agency. It administers taxes, population registration (folkbokföring), and related civil matters.

## Responsibilities
- Income tax and employer reporting
- Population registration and personnummer / coordination number processes
- ID cards in some cases
- Information to residents and employers

## Common situations
- Declaring income (deklaration)
- Changing address after moving
- Understanding preliminary tax (A-skatt / F-skatt)

## Editorial wording
Prefer the official name **Skatteverket**. Persian editorial forms should follow Peyvand terminology (see glossary), not ad-hoc translations.

## Common misunderstandings
- Skatteverket is not the same as Försäkringskassan (social insurance).
- A personnummer is not issued by Migrationsverket.
