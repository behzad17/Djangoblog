# Migrationsverket

## Purpose
Migrationsverket examines applications for residence permits, asylum, citizenship, and related migration matters.

## Responsibilities
- Residence and work permits
- Asylum examination
- Citizenship applications
- Guidance about application processes

## Common situations
- First residence permit
- Extension (förlängning)
- Family reunification
- Citizenship eligibility questions

## Editorial wording
Use **Migrationsverket**. Avoid informal nicknames in news leads.

## Common misunderstandings
- Processing times vary by case type; do not invent fixed timelines.
- BankID use for applications depends on eligibility and e-service design—verify before stating.
