# Region

## Purpose
Swedish **regions** are responsible for healthcare and regional development, including hospitals and primary care organisation.

## Responsibilities
- Healthcare provision (sjukvård)
- Public transport in many regions (often via regional companies)
- Regional planning

## Common situations
- Finding a vårdcentral
- Understanding referral pathways
- Using 1177 for health information

## Editorial wording
Distinguish **Region** (e.g. Region Stockholm) from municipality (kommun).

## Common misunderstandings
- Healthcare access rules can depend on registration and insurance status—verify for the reader’s situation.

## References
- 1177.se; regional websites
