# Healthcare in Sweden

## Summary
Healthcare is largely organised by regions. Primary care often starts at a vårdcentral; 1177 provides health information and guidance.

## Key points for editors
- Explain vårdcentral vs hospital emergency care.
- Avoid medical advice; stick to system navigation facts.
