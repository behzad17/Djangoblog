# Education in Sweden

## Summary
Sweden’s education system includes preschool, compulsory school, upper secondary (gymnasium), adult education, and higher education. SFI supports Swedish language learning for eligible adults.

## Key points for editors
- Distinguish SFI from higher education admissions.
- CSN is about student finance, not admissions.
