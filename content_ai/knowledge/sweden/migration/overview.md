# Migration in Sweden

## Summary
Migration matters in Sweden are primarily handled by Migrationsverket, with related roles for other authorities depending on the case.

## Key points for editors
- Separate residence permit, work permit, asylum, and citizenship topics clearly.
- Do not invent processing times; cite official ranges or say they vary.
- Prefer official Swedish terms with Persian editorial equivalents from the glossary.
