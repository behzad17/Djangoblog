# Editorial Guidelines

## Scope
Standards for accuracy, fairness, sourcing, and community responsibility.

## Rules
- Verify agency claims against primary sources when possible.
- Label community experience as such.
- Protect reader safety (no requests for secrets, codes, or sensitive uploads).
