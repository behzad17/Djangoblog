# Peyvand Editorial Glossary

Official terminology standard (Swedish ↔ Persian). Entries are editorial knowledge, not prompts.

---

## personnummer

- **Swedish term:** personnummer
- **Persian term:** شماره شخصی (editorial; confirm preferred form in review)
- **Alternative Persian wording:** کد هویت شخصی (discouraged if it confuses with other IDs)
- **Definition:** Swedish personal identity number used in many public and private contexts.
- **Editorial recommendation:** Keep Swedish term; explain briefly for newcomers.
- **Example usage:** «برای بسیاری از خدمات به personnummer نیاز دارید.»
- **Related concepts:** coordination number, folkbokföring, Skatteverket
- **References:** skatteverket.se

---

## vårdcentral

- **Swedish term:** vårdcentral
- **Persian term:** مرکز درمانی محلی / وُردسَنترال (editorial choice TBD in review)
- **Alternative Persian wording:** کلینیک محله (informal)
- **Definition:** Primary care centre; usual first contact for non-emergency care.
- **Editorial recommendation:** Prefer Swedish term + short Persian explanation.
- **Example usage:** «اول به vårdcentral مراجعه کنید مگر در شرایط اورژانسی.»
- **Related concepts:** Region, 1177, akuten
- **References:** 1177.se

---

## Skatteverket

- **Swedish term:** Skatteverket
- **Persian term:** اداره مالیات سوئد (descriptive)
- **Alternative Persian wording:** اسکاته ورکت (transliteration)
- **Definition:** Swedish Tax Agency.
- **Editorial recommendation:** Keep official Swedish name in headlines when the agency is the actor.
- **Example usage:** «Skatteverket زمان اظهارنامه را اعلام کرد.»
- **Related concepts:** deklaration, folkbokföring
- **References:** skatteverket.se

---

## Migrationsverket

- **Swedish term:** Migrationsverket
- **Persian term:** اداره مهاجرت سوئد (descriptive)
- **Alternative Persian wording:** میگریشنزورکت (transliteration)
- **Definition:** Swedish Migration Agency.
- **Editorial recommendation:** Official Swedish name first; descriptive Persian optional.
- **Example usage:** «Migrationsverket نتیجه پرونده را اعلام کرد.»
- **Related concepts:** residence permit, citizenship
- **References:** migrationsverket.se

---

## BankID

- **Swedish term:** BankID
- **Persian term:** بانک‌آی‌دی
- **Alternative Persian wording:** هویت دیجیتال بانکی
- **Definition:** Common electronic identification solution used in Sweden.
- **Editorial recommendation:** Never instruct readers to share codes.
- **Example usage:** «بسیاری از خدمات با BankID انجام می‌شود.»
- **Related concepts:** e-services, Swish
- **References:** bankid.com / bank guidance
