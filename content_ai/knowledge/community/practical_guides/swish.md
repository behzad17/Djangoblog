# How Swish Is Commonly Used

## Summary
Swish is a widely used mobile payment service in Sweden for person-to-person and many merchant payments.

## Editorial notes
- Requires a Swedish bank setup compatible with Swish.
- Distinguish Swish from card payments and Bankgiro.
