# How BankID Is Commonly Used

## Summary
BankID is a common electronic ID used to log into banks, public e-services, and many private services.

## Editorial notes
- Not everyone can obtain BankID immediately.
- Never ask readers to share BankID codes or screenshots of security prompts.
