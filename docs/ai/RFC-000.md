# RFC-000 — Peyvand AI Product Vision & Roadmap

**Status:** Accepted (documentation)  
**Type:** Planning & architecture  
**Scope:** Documentation only — no production code, behaviour, or test changes.

---

## Mission

Peyvand AI is **not** a generic text generator.

Peyvand AI is an **Editorial Intelligence Platform** built for the Iranian community living in Sweden.

Its mission is to help editors create **trustworthy, accurate, localised, and high-quality Persian content** while keeping **humans in control** of editorial decisions.

The system should improve continuously while remaining transparent, maintainable, and extensible.

---

## Vision

Build an AI Editorial Platform that assists editors across the full content lifecycle:

| Capability | Role |
|------------|------|
| Research | Orient editors to topics and sources |
| Knowledge retrieval | Ground drafts in Peyvand’s knowledge base |
| Translation & localisation | Swedish context → natural Persian |
| Writing | Drafts and structured suggestions |
| Fact checking | Flag unsupported claims |
| SEO | Intent-aligned titles and structure |
| Editorial review | Checklists and consistency aids |
| Publishing assistance | Workflow support — never auto-publish |
| Analytics | Quality, cost, latency, utilisation |
| Continuous improvement | Learn from editorial feedback |

**Human editors remain responsible for final publication decisions.**

## Core principles

See [PRINCIPLES.md](./PRINCIPLES.md). In short: human-in-the-loop, modular design, separation of responsibilities, transparency, explainability, maintainability, testability, vendor independence, backward compatibility, and incremental evolution.

---

## AI responsibility boundaries

### The AI SHOULD

- Assist editors  
- Suggest improvements  
- Retrieve knowledge  
- Draft articles  
- Explain Swedish concepts  
- Improve readability  
- Generate structured output  

### The AI MUST NOT

- Invent facts  
- Fabricate quotations  
- Publish automatically  
- Replace editorial review  
- Override human decisions
